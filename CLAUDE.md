# Tator's Picks: handoff notes

Private college football ATS pick em for one league. Commissioner: Benton (bperret). Live on Vercel (Hobby) with Neon Postgres. Repo: bperret/Tators-picks.

## Working style
- Plain, short sentences. No em dashes. Lead with the answer. Never end a reply with a question.
- Batch changes. Skip screenshots for wording or logic changes. Run `SETUP_CODE=LOCALCODE npm test` before every push (auto-test plus Playwright, all must pass).
- Commit to main and push; Vercel deploys on push.
- This is a hobby league that will never be commercial. No legal or terms of service comments or searches about it.

## Architecture
- `public/index.html`: the whole page (HTML, CSS, JS in one file). Source of truth.
- `api/`: `state`, `auth`, `picks`, `admin`, `cron` (5 functions). `lib/`: `db`, `http`, `league`, `auto`, `espn`, `teams`, `polls`.
- The server clock enforces the lock (423 after the deadline). Reveals are built server side only after the deadline. Drafts go to the commissioner only.
- Login: cell number plus 6 digit PIN the commissioner makes. 10 wrong tries pause 15 minutes. No texts sent.
- Automation: GitHub Actions set for twice an hour at :19 and :49, but runs often come hours apart (`.github/workflows/automation.yml`, secrets SITE_URL and CRON_SECRET) plus a daily Vercel cron call `/api/cron`. Jobs: preview (Mon 9 a.m. CT to the slate, repulled every 6 hours), slate (Tue 1 p.m. CT from ESPN), refresh (button only, also runs inside open), open (Wed 6 p.m. CT), scores and auto final, rankings (AP, CFP, FPI from ESPN; r/CFB poll from poll.redditcfb.com HTML, `lib/polls.js`), ATS, gamelog (fills weeks 1 to now once, then rechecks the last two weeks Sunday 9 a.m.), lines (fills missing lines and totals from ESPN core per-game odds, 100 games a run, stops if a batch finds none). Failures become flags in the Commish Automation panel. Every job has a manual button. Hand-entered scores are never overwritten.
- Teams has four views. Polls: AP, CFP, r/CFB merged, sortable by any poll (CFP default once out). Power: Ratings (SP+, VSiN, FPI) or Scoring (PPG, PA, margin, SOS from opponents' current SP+; FCS left out). ATS: record, cover margin, O/U; uses the game log record when 5% or fewer of logged games lack a line, else the Covers season record. Tap any team for one shared team card with splits and its game log. Conference and slate filters carry across views. Game log comes from kv `teamGames` through `/api/state?part=teams`.
- Matchup (Teams, item 39, shipped Nov 6): `matchupView(C)`. Two team lists (`muA`, `muH`), a swap button, and a three way home field toggle (team one home, neutral, team two home) in `state.mu` ({a, h, site, from}). Every Picks game and every preview row has a Matchup link (`data-mu`, `muOpen`) that fills both teams, sets home from the game (second team home, neutral site neutral), and shows a Back to this game link; a team picked by hand starts a fresh matchup with neutral as the default. Projections: SP+, FPI, and VSiN rating gap plus home field (`muProject`, margin for team two), their average, the betting line and Robo-Sharp's ESPN FPI game projection when the pair is on the slate or preview (`muGame`). Flags: models disagree by 3 or more, or a model differs from the line by 3 or more (only when the toggle matches the real site). Home field: `HFA`, VSiN's 2026 team values (Adam Burke, Aug 31, 2026: 2 standard, 1 to 3.5 by three year home SU and ATS record), used for all three models since SP+ and FPI publish none (searched Nov 6). Side by side: polls, SP+, FPI, VSiN, record, ATS, cover, points for and against, margin, schedule rank, records vs today's SP+ top 5, top 15, and top 25, home field; better number in green bold. SP+ rank by week reads the weekly snapshots in `state.history` (SP+ only since Nov 2). VSiN shows its raw power number (no sign); SP+ and FPI show plus or minus.
- Record vs ranked teams (team card and Matchup): records against today's SP+ top 5, top 15, and top 25, each tier counting the ones above it (`vs5`, `vs15`, `vs25` in `teamStatRows`). Benton's calls, Nov 6: today's rank predicts, rank at kickoff does not, so the game log does not save ranks; and SP+, not AP or CFP, because it is built to predict rather than reward a résumé. It moves only when Update ratings is tapped. Rows from the brief rank at kickoff build may still hold `ar` and `hr`; nothing reads them.
- Dark mode: follows the device by default. Profile has Auto, Light, Dark, saved per device in localStorage `tp-theme` and applied by an inline script in `<head>` before the page draws (`data-theme` on `<html>`).
- No team logos: they are trademarked marks. Teams show as color chips with abbreviations. USC is "USCw" (USC West) on purpose, to set it apart from South Carolina and to needle USC fans (Benton, Nov 6). Do not "fix" it.
- Commish League members shows each picker's sheet for the week in view: complete, missing parts (picks count, field goal, tiebreaker, survivor if still alive), or not in. The server sends the commissioner game ids and yes or no only (`sheetStatus`), never sides. It is a compact table (`mbBody`, `mbRow`): one row per picker with Sheet and Saved columns, needs attention first (Not in, Missing, Complete). Chips filter it, with counts; removed pickers show only under Removed. Search covers name, nickname, and phone across everyone, whichever chip is on. Tap a row for the Text link (`sms:`), sheet detail, last seen, Reset PIN, and Remove. Remove only hides: picks and profile stay, and Restore brings them back. A removed row also has Start over (two taps, admin action `startOver`): it deletes the picker's picks, pick history, and engagement row, clears name, emoji, and colors, sets `setup_done` false, restores them, and signs them out. Phone and PIN stay, so the next login runs the walk-on. It refuses a picker who is not removed. Add picker is a collapsed control at the top. Filter, search, open row, and the add control live in `state` (`mbFilter`, `mbQ`, `mbOpen`, `mbAdd`), so the 30 second redraw keeps them. `mbPaint` redraws only `#mbBox`, so typing in search keeps focus.
- Do not edit `.github/workflows/automation.yml` from a Claude session. GitHub never fired the schedule while the "claude" account was its last editor; it started after Benton edited the cron line himself (Oct 29). Any change to that file goes to Benton to make on github.com.
- Picks page after the lock: games sort by kickoff; at the same kickoff the better game goes first (AP ranks added, unranked = 26, then best single rank, then slot). The Robo-Sharp model line hides once picks show; Robo-Sharp (🤖 Robo) appears in the pick columns under each game instead, not in the split bar counts.
- Live games: the scores job saves `period`, `clock`, `dclock`, `at` per game and `scoresAt` on the week, and saves clock-only changes without a log entry. The picks page shows "Q3 · 8:42" (Halftime, OT, End of Q2, Final) and "Scores updated" time. Your live pick gets a tier chip with cover odds: normal curve, sd = 13.5 x sqrt(minutes left / 60). Safe 90%+, Leaning 70%+, Toss up 40%+, In danger 15%+, Red alert below. Scores refresh on each scheduled run, plus page visit runs when that switch is on.
- Cover badges on game cards read from your side: green "You covered" or "You're covering" on your pick, red "Beat you" or "Beating you" on the other team. No pick keeps the neutral label.
- Result reveal: `revealFinals()` runs after each refresh (the page polls every 60 seconds). A card and flash show games that went final since this device last looked, saved per login in localStorage `tp-seen-finals-<uid>`. Several finals share one card. When the picker's field goal is among them, the card leads with it (item 50): "It's good! +3" in gold (`fggood`) or "No good." in gray (`fgno`), with the goalpost icon.
- Sealed sheet (item 49, shipped Nov 11): `revealFinals` opens `showSealed` instead of the card when the week is final and 2 or more of your picks are fresh, or 4 or more are fresh at once. This replaced the old rule that a first visit after a week closed stayed quiet. Your picks show face down (`.seal`, slot order), tap to flip (`sealFlip`); the field goal is last, wide, and stays disabled until the rest are flipped. Flip all turns the rest, then the field goal after 0.7 seconds. The field goal flip runs `fgMoment` (new flash, gold or gray card, head "It's good! +3" or "No good."). The summary (`sealFinal`) gives points and record, plus "You won the week. The belt is yours." and "Hot week" when true. No auto close. Seen is still per login, per device (`tp-seen-finals-<uid>`), so a second device sees it again.
- The belt (item 51): `beltOf()` is the winner of the latest final week this season (`weekWinners`, shared winners all hold it, never Robo-Sharp). `beltTag(pid)` draws the gold belt SVG (`BELT`) in the season table's marks column and on the Pickers profile name; `beltLine()` is a notice on Picks (not for rookies or drafts). Since Nov 12 the belt is also the icon for every weekly title (weekly board awards column, Weekly titles panel), so several pickers can show a belt there; that is fine (Benton). The trophy is gone from Standings.
- Picks notices (Nov 12): `notice(icon, html, cls)` draws one row with an icon on the left. What you need (🧮), the rival line (👿), the belt (`BELT`), and Sheets in (📝, full names, no "hidden until the lock" line) sit in one `.notices` grid with `grid-auto-rows:1fr`, so every row is as tall as the tallest. Same colors as a panel. The "1 to 5 / 6 to 10" legend sits under the 10 games, above the tiebreaker.
- Header (Nov 12): the open week reads "Week N · Picks Lock On Sat ..."; the status badge is a full width bar (`.field-row .badge`).
- Weekly board awards column (Nov 12): a column between Picker and Pts. The belt for the week's winner, 🤏 (pinch, "so close") for second: the best score and tiebreaker after the winners, ties share, never Robo-Sharp.
- Removed pickers (Nov 12): pickers never see them on Standings (season table, weekly board, Weekly titles). The commissioner still sees them crossed out at the bottom of the season table.
- Hot hand (item 56, moved to 2026 Nov 11): `HOT_MIN` = 7 correct picks in a final week is a hot week (🔥); 2 or more straight hot weeks is a heater (🔥×N). Counted back from the latest final week this season, so a hot week shows until the next week goes final and a missed or cold week ends it. Void games do not change the bar. Robo-Sharp gets neither. `hotOf`, `hotTag`.
- Season table marks: Iron Man, belt, and hot hand sit in their own column between Picker and Pts (`marksH`, `td.aw`), wide enough for 3 marks. On phones 420 px and under the marks wrap to two rows and names wrap instead of cutting off, so the table fits a 360 px screen with no sideways scroll (tested). A key line under the table explains each mark that shows. Pct is a whole percent (`pctText`).
- Weekly titles (Nov 12): `titlesPanel` lists one row per picker (belt, chip, name, then the weeks won, comma separated), most weekly titles first, then Conf Champ. No "Most titles" line. Beat the bot shows picker chips.
- Full names (Nov 12, Benton): pickers always show by full name. `first(id)` now returns the full name (kept so callers stay the same); never shorten a name. Pick columns under each game wrap long names (`.wn`).
- Survivor picks by week (Nov 12): oldest week on the left, the open week at the right end.
- Survivor tab header (Nov 12): "💀 Survivor 💀", a one line tagline, and 🟢 alive, 🪦 out, 🗓️ weeks played.
- Standings Survivor box (Nov 12): 🟢 alive and 💀 out pills, then the alive pickers as chips when 12 or fewer.
- Rank arrows (item 53): `rankMoves(ids)` ranks the active pickers through all season weeks but the latest counted week, then through all of them, with the default table's ranking (counted points, field goals, tiebreaker; ties share). ▲N, ▼N, or "new" (no sheet before) beside the # (`moveTag`). Shown only in the default view (Current points before the drop counts, Worst week dropped after), never for Robo-Sharp. During a live week they move with the live score.
- Near miss (item 52): `nearMiss(wid)` adds up to two lines to Your week on the Recap for a final week: tied for the top score but lost the tiebreaker (with both misses); a field goal that missed by 1.5 or less when good would have won or tied the top score; one point from the top score, with "Half a point from tying" when the closest miss was 0.5; and "One correct pick from a hot week" at 6 correct.
- Week dropdown (item 40): Standings has a Week select (`#stWeekSel`) over the weekly board when the season has 2 or more revealed weeks. `state.stWeek`, not saved, so a reload returns to the latest week. Weeks not final show "(in progress)".
- Weekly recap: Recap tab, built in the page by `recapFacts(wid)` from saved picks and results, for final weeks only, with a week picker. A banner on Picks shows until the latest recap is read (`tp-recap-seen-<uid>`). Clean jokes, referee voice. A negative award never lands on the same picker twice in a week. To grow it, add facts to `recapFacts`.

- Payouts: `#payouts` in Rules, `PAY` holds the shares, `payUpdate()` does the math, and the shares total 100 at 13 weeks and 4 runs. Page only; nothing is saved.
- Picker stats on the Pickers tab: career wins (`careerPanel`), tendencies (`insightPanel`), past picks by week (`pastPanel`). All read a data object D from `realStats()`, so tests can feed them a dummy league (see `dev/test.mjs`). Robo-Sharp gets none of these. Bowl and CFP wins read `bowl` and `cfp` in `realStats()`, empty until those contests exist.
- Beat the close: `closingLines` in `lib/league.js` sends `state.closing` (saved last line before kickoff, half-point rule applied, started games only). `closeOf` compares it to `homeLine`. Moved games only; `closePanel` is on Standings, with Robo-Sharp unranked. The game log is read only if a started game has no saved line, since the page polls every 60 seconds.
- Commish sections fold (details with `data-sec`), saved per device in localStorage `tp-commish-open`, plus Expand all and Collapse all. Defaults: This week and Swap games open; Automation opens when it has flags; League members opens when a new PIN is showing.

- SP+ and VSiN: the "ratings" job (`lib/ratings.js`), button only (Update ratings in Automation, never in `dueJobs`). SP+ reads the ratings table on ESPN's "2026 college football SP+ rankings for all 138 FBS teams" story (`SP_URL`, skips the résumé table, maps ESPN short names). VSiN finds Adam Burke's "Week N ... Power Ratings and Projections for Every Game" article from the VSiN college page, then the usual address; N is the coming week (current week + 1 once it locks). Checks: 130+ known schools, page under 10 days old, average change from saved under 5 points; else a flag and nothing saved. Each source saves on its own. Unchanged numbers report "has not changed". Paste fallback: a power-only JSON in Manual backups merges the same way. Both sources post Sunday or Monday; CFB Update lagged, so it is not used.
- Preview slate: the `preview` job saves kv `preview` (the slate rule's games and current lines) and no week, so it takes no picks and touches no standings. `buildState` sends `state.preview` to every login: the kv record until a week exists, then the draft's games with no model lines, so swaps and rebuilds show on their own. The commissioner sees the same panel above his draft view. `previewPanel()` sits on top of Picks. ESPN's line coverage on Monday is not verified live; a short preview raises a flag.
- Lines: `refreshDraft` updates a draft's lines, totals, and pool from ESPN with the half-point rule and logs moved lines. It keeps Robo-Sharp's FPI lines. `openJob` runs it first, both on schedule and from either Open picks button, so lines are final when picks open. If ESPN fails, picks still open on saved lines with a flag. The `refresh` job updates the draft, or the preview if no draft exists, and refuses once picks are open. It does not run on a schedule.
- Tab tiles: `GO_TILES` holds 9 stock tiles, one per tab except Commish. `goFoot()` fills `#goFoot`, one footer below all the tab sections, under "Around the league", with every tile but the current tab. It redraws only when the tab changes, since `render()` runs on each poll. No tiles on Commish, and no tile ever links to it. Turf green, so they do not read as warnings. A tile tap lands at the top of the next tab (`state.toTop`). Tab opens count in Engagement as usual; tile taps are not tracked on their own, at Benton's call.

- Page visit runs (`liveRun`, `liveDue` in `lib/auto.js`): `buildState(me, { poll: true })` runs overdue scores or the Wednesday open from a logged in page's state request. The switch `config.liveRun` (`on`, `gap`, `cap`) is set in Automation. It is off in new setups; Benton turned it on for the live site Nov 4. Gap 5 to 60 minutes, default 10. Cap up to 160 a day, default 80. kv `liveRun` holds the day, count, and last attempt, saved behind the database lock before any ESPN call. Scores count only for 12 hours after a game's kickoff, so a postponed game cannot keep calling ESPN. Any 403 or 429 (`espn.takeBlocked`) turns the switch off with a flag. No retries. A quiet poll reads no extra rows, and a poll in a live window reads one small kv row.

## CFP field rules (2026 season, checked Nov 4)
Sources: collegefootballplayoff.com 12 team format page, NCAA.com bracket article of Nov 3, Sports Illustrated on the Group of Six change.
- Field: 12 teams from the committee's final rankings, Sun Dec 6.
- Automatic bids: the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, and SEC champions, plus the highest ranked team from the Group of Six (AAC, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12, Sun Belt). That team does not have to be a champion.

- At-large: the highest ranked other teams fill the field to 12.
- Seeding: straight by committee rank among the 12. A low ranked automatic bid drops to the bottom seeds. No conference champion byes.

- First round: No. 12 at No. 5, No. 11 at No. 6, No. 10 at No. 7, No. 9 at No. 8. The higher seed hosts, on campus or at a site it names. Dec 18 and 19.
- Quarterfinals: No. 1 plays the 8/9 winner, No. 2 the 7/10 winner, No. 3 the 6/11 winner, No. 4 the 5/12 winner. No reseeding. No changes to avoid rematches or teams from the same conference. Seeds pick bowls in order, 1 first and 4 last. Fiesta Dec 30; Cotton, Peach, and Rose Jan 1.
- Semifinals: Orange Jan 14, Sugar Jan 15. Title: Mon Jan 25, Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas.
- Prototype assumption: the highest ranked team in each conference is its champion. It shows a warning for a ranked team with no conference, or a missing automatic bid.
