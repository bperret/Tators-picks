Rules

The slate

  • Each week has 10 Saturday games that kick off at 11 a.m. Central or later. Slots 1 to 5 are the top games by AP rank. A game between two ranked teams comes first.
  • Slots 6 to 10 are the five closest spreads among the other games with a ranked team. On a tie, the better ranked team wins the slot.
  • The commissioner may swap any game before picks open. Each swap shows on the slate. The slate does not change after picks open.

The lines

  • Lines come from DraftKings, as ESPN shows them when the slate posts. They do not move after that.
  • Every line is a half point, so no game can push. When the posted line is a whole number, the underdog gets the extra half point.

Your picks

  • Pick a side in each game against the spread. A correct pick is 1 point. A wrong or missed pick is 0.
  • Throw your yellow flag on one game you picked. If that pick is correct, it scores 3 points instead of 1.
  • Guess the total points in the tiebreaker game, slot 1. No guess counts as a guess of 0.
  • All picks lock Saturday at 10:15 a.m. Central, before the first kickoff. After the lock, the site refuses every save. The site checks its own clock, not your phone's.
  • Every save goes into your pick history with the time. After the lock, everyone can see everyone's picks and history.
  • Nobody sees other picks until the lock.

Scoring

  • A postponed or cancelled game is void and scores for nobody. A flag on a void game earns nothing.
  • If slot 1 is void, slot 2 becomes the tiebreaker game.
  • Weekly winner: most points, then the closest tiebreaker guess. If it is still tied, the title is shared. The weekly title is for bragging rights only.
  • Season: most total points, then most flag wins, then the smallest total of tiebreaker misses. Test weeks do not count.
  • Bowl games are a separate contest.

Logins

  • You log in with your cell number and a PIN. The commissioner gives you your first PIN.
  • You can change your PIN on your Profile after you log in. Only the commissioner can reset a forgotten PIN.
  • After 10 wrong PINs in a row, that number pauses for 15 minutes. Text the commissioner if you need in sooner.
  • Your cell number is never shown to other players.

Robo-Sharp

  • Robo-Sharp is a computer player. Each week it takes the side the model line favors against the betting line in every game, and throws its flag on the game with the biggest gap. The model line is VSiN's projected spread.
  • Robo-Sharp shows in the standings so you can see who beats the computer. It holds no rank and cannot win a week, the season, or money.

Survivor

  • Each week, also pick one team from the slate against the spread. If it covers, you stay alive. If it does not, you are out.
  • You cannot pick the same team twice. A void game keeps you alive. A missed survivor pick puts you out.
  • Survivor picks lock and reveal with your regular picks. The last player alive wins. If the final players go out in the same week, they share it.
  • The Survivor tab shows every player's pick each week, who is still alive, and which teams you have left.

Money and players

  • Buy ins and payouts happen outside this page.
  • The commissioner removes any player who has not paid. A removed player cannot pick and shows greyed out in the standings.

Full disclosure: the commissioner runs the site and could read the database before the lock. The site never shows picks to anyone before the lock.
